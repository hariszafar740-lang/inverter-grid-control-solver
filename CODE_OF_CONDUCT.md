# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability.
Ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

* Using welcoming, constructive, and technically rigorous language
* Respecting differing engineering viewpoints and analytical critiques
* Accepting responsibility for mistakes and focusing on what is best for the community

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported via GitHub Issues or private security advisories on this repository.
All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
